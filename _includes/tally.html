Rules, fixed before the first call

  1. Every prediction states four things: the claim, a resolution date, a confidence as a number, and what would prove it wrong.
  2. Confidence is a percentage, never a word. "Likely" cannot be scored.
  3. Resolution runs against a named public source, chosen when the call is made, not when it resolves.
  4. Nothing is deleted or quietly reworded. Resolutions are appended, never written over the claim.
  5. A call that cannot be wrong is not published.
  6. Roughly one essay in four carries a call. No call is better than a forced one.
