Research Log on Industries, Workflows & Predictions

Four kinds of writing, in increasing order of risk.

Analysis — how an industry actually works: the workflow underneath the headlines, and the impact that workflow creates. Published weekly.

Research — how an industry could change that impact by changing the workflow. One variable moved, assumptions on the table.

Predictions — what a given change should produce, and by when. Dated, scored, never deleted.

Thesis — industries and workflows I've designed myself, where I've measured the impact and believe it would be large.

Analysis runs every week. Research, Predictions and Thesis appear only when I have real conviction — one essay a week, no exceptions.
