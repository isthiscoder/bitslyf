Five industries decide what the next fifty years physically can and cannot do: semiconductors,
energy, artificial intelligence, space and defence. They are not five stories. They are one story
about physical constraint — fabrication, power, compute, lift and deterrence.

> Software ate the world, then discovered the world has a supply chain.

Most coverage of these sectors is financial or promotional. Very little explains the mechanism:
where capacity comes from, how long it takes to build, who owns the single-vendor chokepoints,
and which constraint binds first.

Predictions concentrate in semiconductors and energy. Analysis ranges wider — a record spread
across twenty industries could never tell skill from luck.
