# Bitslyf

I do love Research for that i started Bitslyf as a side Project. The name says what it does. Bits are the small, fundamental pieces of how industries actually work. Lyf is how industries evolve, and the new possibilities they create. Bitslyf is the bridge between the two: from analysing how an industry runs today to imagining what it becomes next. Every piece connects. We start with Analysis, move to Research, make Predictions and form a Thesis, then put it all together into Requests for Startups RFS: ideas builders can turn into companies. Every prediction is scored in public on the Scorecard. Go ahead with [Bitslyf](https://isidd.dev). Author - Siddharth singh.
